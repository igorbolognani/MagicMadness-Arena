# D3 — Fire, Water, Earth & Air

> **MAGICMADNESS ARENA — CANONICAL PRODUCT DEFINITION**
>
> Status: **implementation baseline / frozen unless explicitly changed by Igor**.
> This file translates the approved 46-point decision set into an implementable specification.
> Do not reopen product strategy during implementation. Unfixed numeric values may be calibrated,
> but the mechanic, progression philosophy, fairness rule and interaction model remain canonical.

## Purpose

The four starter elements are:
- onboarding;
- first History progression;
- first free hero families;
- first proof that element changes mechanics.

## First choice

Player chooses one:
- Fire;
- Water;
- Earth;
- Air.

This is the first progression focus, not a permanent account lock.

The other starter families are obtainable through play.

## Teaching matrix

| Starter | Primary teaching |
|---|---|
| Fire | projectile, explosion, knockback |
| Water | push/pull, flow, slow/manipulation |
| Earth | tanking, wall, mass, stability |
| Air | movement, trajectory, recovery |

## History sequence

```text
FIRE CHAPTER
 → direct cast
 → explosion/knockback
 → hazard positioning
 → bosses

WATER CHAPTER
 → push/pull
 → flow/slow
 → moving interaction
 → bosses

EARTH CHAPTER
 → walls/cover
 → mass/stability
 → object interaction
 → bosses

AIR CHAPTER
 → mobility
 → redirection
 → edge recovery
 → bosses
```

Each chapter can contain more than one boss.

## Boss standard

A boss may use:
- up to 5 active authored skills/mechanics;
- phase behavior;
- map mechanic hooks;
- summons;
- high but balanced life;
- explicit telegraphs.

Skill and mechanics mastery must matter more than paying.

## Mechanical identities

### Fire
Explosion, radial impulse, ignition/heat zones and reactions.

### Water
Push, pull, flow fields, movement manipulation.

### Earth
Mass, stability, walls, terrain/object interaction.

### Air
Mobility, gusts, trajectory redirection, edge recovery.

## Free progression promise

Early PvE must provide a practical path to:
- progress chosen starter;
- acquire/progress the other three;
- access foundational talents/runes;
- earn collection/gacha resources.

Exact quantities belong to D11.

## Competitive promise

Starter heroes are never intentionally weaker than later gacha heroes.

Later heroes bring different mechanics, complexity and playstyles.

## Acceptance tests

- player identifies four starter identities quickly;
- every chapter teaches a mechanics family;
- each major boss has at least one non-DPS mechanic;
- all starters can win equal-budget PvP;
- core systems remain accessible through play.
